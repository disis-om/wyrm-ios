# Wyrm — Privacy

Last updated 23 October 2026. Wyrm is made by OM Rajput. This is the iPhone
edition of the same document the Android app carries; the parts that differ are
the ones about how you sign in and where secrets are kept.

This is written to be read, not to be scrolled past. If something here is
unclear, that is a fault worth reporting.

---

## The short version

- On iPhone you sign in with a **username and a password** you choose. Wyrm does
  not ask Apple, Google or anyone else who you are.
- What Wyrm stores about you is what you can already see in the app: your
  names, your picture, your best score, your kills, and anything you typed into
  chat.
- Nothing is sold. Nothing is shared with advertisers. There are no adverts and
  no tracking libraries in this app.
- You can delete your account from inside the app, and it deletes.

---

## Signing in

Your password is sent once, over an encrypted connection, to Wyrm's own server
when you create the account or sign in. The server keeps only a salted hash of
it, never the password itself. What the app keeps afterwards is a session token,
and it lives in the iPhone **Keychain**, not in ordinary app storage.

## What Wyrm knows about you

### What you give it

| What | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Your username | It is the `@handle` other players find you by, and how you sign in |
| Your display name | Shown on your profile and in lists |
| Your profile picture | Your avatar, only if you add one |

- **An in-game name**, printed over your snake in the arena.
- **A short bio**, if you write one.

### What playing produces

- Your **highest score** and **total kills**, and a record of each finished run
  so those totals can be recounted rather than trusted blindly.
- **Messages** you send in global chat or in a direct thread, and **reports** you
  file about someone else's message.
- **Who you follow** and **who you have blocked**.
- **Name changes**, so the limit on how often you may rename is counted from
  something a reinstall cannot reset.
- If you choose Voice Chat, a separately verified **email address** is stored
  encrypted for voice access and abuse prevention. Other players and the
  operator dashboard never see it.
- Voice rooms keep creator, membership, moderation and call-duration metadata.
  Wyrm does not store the audio, record it or transcribe it.
- **Your settings.** Controls, layout, colours, sizes, themes, your skin and
  look, and your notification choices are saved to your account when the app
  goes to the background and when you log out, so they come back when you log
  in on any phone. iPhone and Android each keep their own copy; your skin and
  look are shared between them. They are deleted with your account.
- **When you play, while Wyrm is open on your screen.** The app tells the
  server what you are doing as it changes: on the menus, in the Ready Room,
  playing in an arena (with that arena's address), playing with AI, or idle
  after two minutes without a touch. It stops when the app goes to the
  background. Each finished run also carries how long it lasted and the arena.
  This is how friends see that you are online and where you play, and how the
  operator sees how many people play and for how long. It is deleted after 90
  days, and with your account.

### What stays on your phone and never leaves it

- Your Team Mode credentials, if you use Team Mode. These are held in the iOS
  Keychain and are sent only to the team service they belong to, never to
  Wyrm's server.
- Developer logs, only if you turn Developer Mode on. They expire after seven
  days and leave the phone only if you share them yourself.
- Logging out removes your settings, messages and Team credentials from the
  phone, so nothing of your account is left there for the next person.

## Who else is involved

Wyrm talks to outside services, and it is worth knowing which is which:

1. **Slither's public arena directory**, to list which arenas exist and how busy
   they are, and then the arena itself while you play. This is the game you are
   playing; your snake's position goes to the arena server the way it does in
   any online game. Wyrm sends no account information there — the arena knows
   only the name over your snake.
   Community-run arenas: Wyrm keeps a random 16-character arena id for your
   account (random, not made from your name or e-mail) and sends it
   only to the arenas on a public list of community-run arenas, so those arenas
   can recognise you between games. Official slither arenas never get it.
2. **The team service**, only if you turn on Team Mode, and only with the
   credentials you entered yourself.
3. **Brevo**, only to deliver the Voice Chat verification code that Wyrm's
   backend generated.
4. **Snakey Rain**, only when you enable it in Loadout. The app sends your
   public or personal Rain login, chosen characters, arena address, a copy of
   your arena join, snake position, target and control commands directly to
   `snakeyrain.com` over an encrypted connection. This service has its own
   operator. Personal login and private bot tag codes stay in this phone's
   Keychain, are sent only to Rain, and are cleared on Wyrm logout. Rain settings
   and non-secret characters follow your Wyrm account. Turning Rain off or
   leaving the app disconnects it and requests bot stop. Creating a personal
   Rain account opens that service's website in a browser.

Wyrm's own server is a machine run by the author. It is not a cloud
account with an analytics dashboard attached.

## What Wyrm does not do

- No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
- No analytics or crash-reporting service from anyone else. Wyrm's own server
  keeps when you play (described above), and nothing else about your sessions.
- No location or contacts, and no photo library scanning. The app asks for a
  photo only when you pick one, and only that photo.
- No voice recording and no transcription.
- No selling, renting or sharing of your data. There is nobody to sell it to.

## Other players see

Your display name, your username, your in-game name, your avatar, your bio, your
best score and your kills, and anything you posted in global chat. Friends (players who follow you and whom you follow
back) see whether you are on Wyrm right now and which arena you play in or last
played in. Signed-in players also see you in the arena chat's **Online** list
for a few minutes after you use Wyrm: your in-game name, your username and the
arena you played in. The same activity (whether you are on Wyrm, the arena you
play in, your in-game name and username) can also appear on the Wyrm website
and in the Wyrm Discord bot. This sharing is always on and cannot be turned
off (since 2026-10-23; the old **Show my activity to friends** switch is gone). Your email
address is never shown to another player.

## Deleting messages and Trails

Touch and hold your own message in Global chat or a direct thread, then choose
Delete. Wyrm removes it from the active server database for both sides,
including any report or stored invite notification linked to it, and tells
connected apps to remove it. A deleted message cannot be restored in the app.

Deleting a Trail removes its post, comments, likes, reports and media references.
Its photo, thumbnail and video files are removed from active storage once no
other valid post/upload uses the same file. Deleting a comment also removes its
stored reply notification. Deleting your account removes your Trails, uploaded
Trail media and comments on other Trails.

Disaster-recovery backups and retained rollback releases can still contain old
copies. Nightly database backups rotate after seven days; off-site packs retain
seven daily and four weekly copies. Previous releases remain until release
retention prunes them. Deletion does not recall another player's screenshots,
clipboard, previously delivered notification or downloaded/cached copy.

## Background play

A match can keep running when you switch apps or lock the screen. iPhone plays
a silent sound through a playback audio session while a match is backgrounded,
mixed with other audio, to try to keep the app awake. It stops on return or match
end. The arena connection and bot may continue on a timer without game frames
or GPU drawing. This can use battery and network data; iOS may still suspend or
interrupt the app. No sound is recorded and no microphone access is needed.

## Deleting your account

Profile → Edit profile → Delete account. It removes your account, your photograph and your bio
outright. Messages you already sent stay where they are but are attributed to
"deleted player" — the same way a deleted account works anywhere that people
have already replied to you. Creating an account again starts a new, empty
account; it does not bring the old one back.

## Children

Wyrm is not built for children under 13, and accounts are not knowingly created
for them.
